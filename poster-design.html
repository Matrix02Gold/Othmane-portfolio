Abolution of the Death Penalty

School Project

Poster Design

March 2025

297 mm x 420 mm

This project explores the cruelty of the death penalty, framing it as a relic of the past that stands against true justice, which should respect human dignity and value life.

Through a visual combination of elements historically linked to executions, the work aims to confront viewers with the inhumanity of capital punishment. The core message is clear: we choose compassion over vengeance.
