ALMA

Alma Tangier

Menu Design

June 2025

A3-A5

Alma started from our aim to create a warm and welcoming place in the heart of Tangier. Beyond the food, they created Alma with the aim of gathering a community of people who value good experiences delicious food and quality produce culture and creativity, and sharing in general.


Alma was founded by Lamiae and Seif, a creative duo with a vision, a lot of ambition, and hopes to make a significant impact on Tangier's vibrant food and coffee scene, After years of using there old menus they've decided to take the step into redesigning them.

For this project, I had one question in mind: what does the customer see when they first enter ALMA? Put simply, it is a sophisticated restaurant with a well-thought-out interior design. To translate this into the menu design I chose to simplify these elements into a curated set of colors, using them to reflect and describe the space combined with principles of menu engineering.
