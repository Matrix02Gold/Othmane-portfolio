‘‘Despite the limited production of comic books in Morocco, this art form is striving to establish itself among other artistic mediums’’


AJYAL ‘s vision is ambitious and inspiring! striving to elevate the Moroccan comic book artistry to the global stage akin to marvel ,DC or Shonen jump, is no small.


It tackles these subjects by providing opportunities to the community through seasonal comic competitions offering a chance for selected works to be serialized.


AJYAL follows the traditional comic book format starting with a news section before diving into the latest monthly chapters of serialized comics AJYAL extends to digital platforms, with both a dedicated website and a mobile application.


You might see it as just a comic book magazine but the way I see it, it has the power to shape future generations.

In the early stages of AJYAL’s development, I envisioned it as a magazine with a presence in both digital and print formats.
