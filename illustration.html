Hermes

DR EL Mehdi Boumchita

Illustration

February 2024

Mural

Hermes, in ancient Greek mythology, is the messenger of the gods, associated with movement, communication, and guidance. His symbolic staff, the caduceus featuring two intertwined serpents—has often been linked to the medical field as a representation of healing and balance.


For this project, Hermes serves as a dynamic and symbolic figure, introduced through a mural to break the sterile and monotonous atmosphere of the dental clinic, bringing energy storytelling, and a sense of curiosity into the space.
